THE NORTH STAR METHOD

Three pillars. One journey.

The North Star Method is built around three simple ideas.

First, you'll discover your North Star—a clear vision of where you feel called to go and the direction your soul is guiding you toward.

Next, we'll create a personalized Roadmap, transforming that vision into practical goals and meaningful action.

Finally, you'll strengthen your Inner Compass, learning to recognize and trust your own intuition so you can continue navigating your path with clarity and confidence.

Throughout all eight sessions, I work together with your spirit guides while helping you deepen your own connection and develop greater trust in your intuitive abilities.
